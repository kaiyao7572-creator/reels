Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy covers the publishing tools of the Kaidaidkslop channel: the Meta app "igapi", used by the Instagram account @kaidaidkslop, and the Google Cloud app "kaidaidk", used by the YouTube channel Kaidaidkslop. Both are private tools. They manage only the channel's own accounts, which belong to their developer, and are not offered to anyone else.

What the apps do

  • Publish the channel's own videos to @kaidaidkslop on Instagram and to the Kaidaidkslop channel on YouTube.
  • Read the list of the channel's own posts and uploads, to number episodes and to avoid posting the same video twice.
  • Read comments on @kaidaidkslop's own Instagram posts, so the account owner can reply to them, hide them or delete them.

Google and YouTube data

The YouTube app asks Google for two permissions: to upload videos to the Kaidaidkslop channel (youtube.upload) and to read that channel's own list of uploads (youtube.readonly). It uses them only for the two things above. The sign-in token is stored only on the account owner's own computer. The app does not read any other channel, any viewer's data or any private data of the Google account.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The apps also follow the YouTube Terms of Service; see the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data.

Instagram comment data

When it reads comments, the Instagram app receives what Instagram provides for each comment: the commenter's username, the comment text and the time it was posted. This is kept only on the account owner's own computer, to keep track of which comments have been answered. Comment text may be processed by a language model that runs on that same computer, to suggest a reply.

What the apps do not do

  • They do not sell, share or rent any data, and send no data to any service other than Instagram, YouTube and Google sign-in.
  • They do not use data for advertising or profiling.
  • They do not access any account other than the channel's own.

Deleting your data

If you commented on @kaidaidkslop and want any stored copy of your comments deleted, send a direct message to @kaidaidkslop on Instagram asking for deletion. Your comments will be removed from the apps' local records within 30 days. Deleting a comment on Instagram itself also stops the app from seeing it. The channel owner can revoke the YouTube app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which also makes the stored token useless.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
